HRS Group Home/Group Heritage & Governance
Corporate Heritage • Ahmedabad & Bengaluru

Built with patience, engineered for generational scale.

Headquartered in Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, HRS Group builds and operates foundational enterprises in the physical economy: high-rise architectural facades, residential communities, modern retail, spice milling, and hospitality stays.

Our origins in Gujarat’s industrial heartland

Founded in Ahmedabad, HRS Group began as a regional real estate development firm focused on planned residential communities across western Gujarat. Over two decades, disciplined capital allocation and direct operating control enabled expansion from land development into architectural building envelopes, advanced manufacturing, and consumer distribution.

Today, our manufacturing facilities in Sanand fabricate advanced unitised curtain walls for commercial towers across western and southern India, while our food processing mill in Bavla blends and packages spices to international food safety standards. Each venture operates with dedicated sector leadership while drawing on shared group systems.

We remain an owner-led enterprise committed to long-term physical assets, dependable commercial contracts, and community trust. From neighbourhood retail destinations to technical education, our businesses are built to serve enduring everyday needs.

Chronology of enterprise

Milestones from 2012 to present

2012

Founding of HRS Group in Ahmedabad

Inception of HRS Aluglaze in Ahmedabad by Rupesh Shah and Hrishikesh Shah, pioneering engineered architectural aluminium fenestration.

2015

Real Estate Expansion under Hrishikesh Ventures

Launched planned residential developments and integrated commercial centers across key growth nodes in western Ahmedabad.

2018

Bavla Botanical & Spice Milling Campus

Commissioned a dedicated low-temperature stone milling facility in Bavla to process single-origin clean spices and seasonal blends.

2020

Acquisition of Geotrix in Bengaluru

Established direct Southern India facade engineering and site installation capabilities across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana.

2022

School of Facade Technologies Academy

Founded dedicated technical institute to bridge structural engineering theory and hands-on site installation mastery.

2024

Launch of FirstGen & BOGO Retail Ecosystem

Unified commercial asset management under FirstGen and unveiled BOGO multi-format retail destinations across Gujarat.
