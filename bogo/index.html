Intelligent retail infrastructure

Six planned capability phases designed to remove friction, enhance product discovery, and streamline everyday checkout.

01Planned

Purchase History

A connected shopping history in the BOGO app that helps customers review past purchases, track recurring household expenditure, and re-order pantry staples quickly.

Phase 1 roadmapBOGO Tech
02Planned

Smart Basket

An intelligent shopping basket that automatically tallies selected items and synchronises with the customer’s active shopping list in real time.

Phase 2 roadmapBOGO Tech

Concierge Service

Patrons can browse inventory and place advance pre-orders, arriving at their neighbourhood store simply to pick up pre-packed groceries.
